About The Project

What is AI Consent Guardian?

An AI-powered privacy analysis app that gives everyday internet users clarity before clicking "I Agree". It parses dense legal documents and extracts data practices, permissions, and red flags into simple visual dashboards.

1. Artificial Intelligence

Leverages cutting-edge Large Language Models (Google Gemini API) for zero-shot legal text analysis, entity extraction, sentiment scoring, and natural language question answering.

2. Cybersecurity & Privacy

Promotes user consent awareness and data sovereignty. Flags hidden telemetry, aggressive ad tracking, indefinite data retention, and non-consensual third-party broker sharing.

3. Internet Programming

Engineered with Python & Flask, asynchronous Fetch API, secure document processing via PyPDF, and a responsive animated light-mode interface using AOS & modern CSS.
